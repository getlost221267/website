FAQ

Which Minecraft version do the clients support?

The version is shown in the top right corner of each client. Most are made for 1.21.11.

A download doesn't open. What now?

Downloads open in Google Drive. Sign in to Drive, then choose Download from the menu in the top right.

What does COMING SOON mean?

That client isn't released yet. The button stays locked until it is.

How do I report a broken link?

Send us a message on Discord. You can find it under Socials.
